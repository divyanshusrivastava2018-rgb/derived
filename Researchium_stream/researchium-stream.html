Platform capabilities

Everything researchers need

From single-researcher livestreams to global multi-institution conferences — one platform, every tool.

📡
Multi-platform broadcasting

Stream simultaneously to YouTube, Twitch, LinkedIn, Facebook, and 30+ more platforms with a single click. No bandwidth overhead on your end.

🔬
Research Studio

Browser-based production suite purpose-built for science — overlays for data visualisation, equation rendering, molecular structures, and live graphs.

💬
Unified peer Q&A

Aggregate chat from all destinations into one moderated feed. Upvote questions, pin important peer insights, and respond without switching tabs.

🤖
AI transcription & clipping

Auto-generate timestamped transcripts and highlight reels from every session. 99-language support for global collaboration.

🔒
Secure gated sessions

Optional gated sessions with room tokens and TLS in production. RTMPS to destinations; WebRTC signaling uses short-lived JWTs (see Architecture).

📊
Audience analytics

Cross-platform watch time, drop-off points, geographic reach, and citation impact scoring — all in a single research dashboard.

System design

From UI to CDN

Next.js and WebRTC up front; Socket.IO signaling, mediasoup SFU, FFmpeg ABR, HLS, and NGINX at the edge.

Create your research channel

          
Sign up and build your researcher profile — add your institution, ORCID, research domains, and co-authors. Your channel becomes your public broadcast identity.

            DOI linking
            Institution badge
          

        

      

      

        
02

        

          
Connect your platforms

          
Link your YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitch, Facebook, or any custom RTMP endpoint in one click. Researchium Stream fans your signal out to all destinations in real time.

          

            30+ platforms
            Custom RTMP
            OAuth one-click
          

        

      

      

        
03

        

          
Set up your stream

          
Use Research Studio in your browser, or connect OBS, vMix, or any RTMP encoder. Add your lab camera, screen share, slides, data overlays, and invite remote co-presenters via link.

          

            Browser studio
            OBS/vMix/Ecamm
            Guest invite link
            Data overlays
          

        

      

      

        
04

        

          
Go live & engage your audience

          
Hit broadcast. All platforms go live simultaneously. Manage your unified Q&A feed, respond to peer questions, and watch real-time analytics across channels from one dashboard.

          

            One-click broadcast
            Unified chat
            Live analytics
          

        

      

      

        
05

        

          
Archive & share your findings

          
Every session is automatically transcribed, chaptered, and saved. AI generates highlight clips, and the full recording is indexed for citation and DOI assignment through our academic partners.
