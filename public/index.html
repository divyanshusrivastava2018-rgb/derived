Researchium is an online learning platform for science exam preparation — courses, live classes, study PDFs, and mock tests for CSIR NET, GATE, IIT-JAM, JEE, NEET, and UPSC aspirants in one place.

Yes — the free tier includes topic-wise study PDFs, sample mock tests, and select video lectures. Subscribe to unlock live classes, full mock series, and premium course uploads.
What is included in a subscription?+
Plans include live class access, premium course uploads, full mock test packs, and downloadable study materials. See the pricing page for current tiers.

Yes — topic quizzes, category mocks, and full papers with timed exam mode, scoring, and detailed explanations after each attempt, covering GATE, CSIR NET, and IIT-JAM patterns.
